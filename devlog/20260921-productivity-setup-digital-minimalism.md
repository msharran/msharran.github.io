# Productivity Setup: Digital Minimalism, Practical Edition

> Note: I consider these devlogs my personal journal of what I'm learning, so I won't be writing a full-fledged article here. Just learnings and thoughts concisely.

<!-- Draft goes here. Key points only, to expand later. -->

- The current setup is intentionally small: a few tools, each doing one job, instead of one big all-in-one system.
- Guiding idea is digital minimalism — fewer apps competing for attention, and each tool should earn its place.
- Practicality over ideology: I keep a tool because it removes friction day-to-day, not because it fits a philosophy on paper.

## Tools in rotation

- **Notion Calendar** — single place for schedule/time-blocking. Replaced a couple of overlapping calendar/task apps.
- **Simple Timer** — plain focus/interval timer. No gamification, no streaks to manage — just start and go.
- **AltTab** — brings proper window switching (with previews) to macOS, closer to what I'm used to elsewhere.
- **Rectangle** — keyboard-driven window snapping/tiling. Removes a lot of manual window dragging.
